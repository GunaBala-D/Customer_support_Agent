Acme Electronics Order Cancellation Policy

When an order can be cancelled
An order can be cancelled free of charge while its status is Pending or Processing. Once an order has the status Shipped it can no longer be cancelled.

If your order has already shipped
You can refuse the delivery or return the package after it arrives. Returns follow the 30-day return policy.

How to cancel
Ask our support assistant or an agent to cancel the order. We always ask you to confirm before an order is cancelled, because a cancellation cannot be undone.

Refunds for cancelled orders
Cancelled orders are refunded in full to the original payment method. The refund is issued within 3 to 5 business days.

Changing an order instead
Items, quantities and delivery address can only be changed while the order is Pending. After that, cancel the order and place a new one.
