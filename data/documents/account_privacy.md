# Account and Privacy

## Resetting your password
Use the "Forgot password" link on the sign-in page. A reset link is emailed to the address on your account and is valid for 60 minutes. Support agents never ask for your password.

## Membership tiers
Acme Electronics has three tiers: standard, plus and premium. Premium members receive a 24-month warranty on eligible products and free express shipping.

## Updating account details
You can change your name, email and phone number in Account Settings. Delivery addresses can be updated in the address book.

## Data retention
We keep order records for 7 years for tax purposes. Chat transcripts are kept for 24 months to improve support quality.

## Deleting your account
You can request account deletion from Account Settings. Personal profile data is erased within 30 days. Order records required for tax purposes are retained in anonymized form.

## Privacy
We never sell personal data. Payment card numbers are processed by our payment provider and are never stored on our servers.
