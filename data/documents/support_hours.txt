Acme Electronics Customer Support Hours and Contact

Support hours
Monday to Friday: 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM Eastern Time.
Saturday: 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM Eastern Time.
Sunday: closed. Email and chat messages are answered the next business day.

Contact channels
Email: support@acme-electronics.example
Phone: 1-800-555-0142 (during support hours)
Live chat: available on the website during support hours, with this assistant available at all times.

Response times
Email requests receive a first response within 1 business day.
Tickets marked high priority, such as payment problems, are reviewed first.

Human escalation
If the assistant cannot resolve your issue it opens a support ticket and a human agent continues the conversation with the full chat history.
