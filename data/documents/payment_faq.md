# Payments FAQ

## Accepted payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Apple Pay. We do not accept cash on delivery, checks or cryptocurrency.

## When is my card charged?
Your card is charged when you place the order. If the payment is authorized but the order is not created, the authorization hold is normally released by your bank within 3 to 5 business days.

## I was charged but have no order confirmation
You should receive a confirmation email within 30 minutes of paying. If you were charged but did not receive a confirmation, a human support agent must investigate the payment. Contact support with the transaction ID or the last four digits of the card used. The assistant cannot create orders or reverse payments.

## Duplicate charges
Occasionally a pending authorization appears twice on a card statement. Duplicate holds drop off automatically. If both charges are still posted after 5 business days, contact support.

## Invoices
An invoice is attached to the order confirmation email. Invoices for past orders can be requested from support.

## Sales tax
Sales tax is calculated at checkout based on the delivery address.
