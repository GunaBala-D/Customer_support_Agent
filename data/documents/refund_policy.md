# Acme Electronics Refund and Return Policy

## Return window
Most products can be returned within 30 days of the delivery date. The 30 days are counted from the day the carrier marks the package as delivered, not from the order date.

## Eligibility
To be eligible for a refund the item must be unused, in its original packaging, and include all accessories. Items that show signs of misuse or damage not present on arrival are not eligible.

Products that cannot be returned: gift cards, downloadable software, personalized or engraved items, and items marked "Final Sale" on the product page.

## Refund method and timing
Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. Refunds appear 5 to 7 business days after our warehouse receives and inspects the returned item. Shipping fees are not refunded unless the return is due to our error.

## Return shipping costs
Return shipping is free when the item is defective or we sent the wrong product. For all other returns a return label costs $6.99, deducted from the refund.

## Restocking fee
Opened laptops, monitors and drones are subject to a 15% restocking fee unless they are defective.

## How to start a return
Contact support with your order number. A support agent will verify eligibility and email you a return label. Do not ship the item before you receive the label.

## Damaged or defective items
If an item arrives damaged or defective, contact us within 14 days of delivery with photos. We will send a replacement or a full refund, including shipping, at no cost to you.
